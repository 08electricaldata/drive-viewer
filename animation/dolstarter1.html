Step-by-step guided notes
Steps:
  1. Choose LOCAL or REMOTE mode.
  2. If LOCAL: press START; if REMOTE: send PLC START.
  3. Observe K1 coil energize, main contacts close and motor run.
  4. Ammeter will rise; transducer converts Amps → 4–20 mA and sends to PLC/DCS.
  5. Inject faults to see protection behavior (MCB OFF, Fuse Open, Trip, etc.).
